CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Delivery, cancellations and refunds

We confirm availability, final price, service details, and any delivery or pickup arrangement with you before an order is completed.

Delivery and pickup

Please contact us to confirm whether delivery is available for your location, the delivery cost, and the expected timing. Pickup timing is confirmed by our team when your order is ready.

Payments

The website records your payment preference but does not collect payments online. We can confirm cash at pickup or delivery, or provide verified Telebirr, CBE, Abay, or Bank of Abyssinia transfer details after your order is confirmed. Do not send passwords or one-time verification codes.

Cancellation

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number if you need to cancel. We will confirm whether work has started or an item has shipped and explain the available options before proceeding.

Returns and refunds

Return or refund eligibility can depend on the item, its condition, and whether custom or repair work has started. Please ask us to confirm the applicable terms for your order before paying or authorizing work. We will discuss the next step with you if there is a problem.
